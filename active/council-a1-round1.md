# Council question

How should a beginner-focused, ADHD-friendly German A1 website be expanded from 46 short lessons and 12 incomplete textbook capsules into substantive in-app teaching of the supplied Netzwerk neu A1 book's complete learning scope over 35 days? Required: pure black UI, lessons grouped sequentially by day, two teaching passes with two explained examples each, three drills, fresh blind transfer, theory plus all four language skills, truthful performance evidence, progress migration, fixed small Credits · Atish. Proposal: 24 teaching days, four platform review days, seven practical application/repair/mock days; roughly 180 focused minutes per day with breaks. The existing app delegates full chapter work to the PDF. Evaluate coverage, retention, beginner clarity, implementation feasibility and acceptance criteria.

## Response A

A beginner is promised a course but currently gets a task coordinator. README explicitly says extended practice happens in the book; sprint-ui.js sends learners to a PDF, publisher site and practice papers. Moving this to 35 days will not fix the missing teaching.

Replace all conflicting 21-day, three-week and 260-minute text with one five-week plan. Show today's focused minutes and next small task. Continue must resume the exact unfinished activity including listening, writing and speaking. Currently, after micro-lessons, Start next lesson can return to the day page without advancing anything. Teach unfamiliar labels such as verb position, target pattern and noun capitals where used. Each task needs instructions, worked examples, answer expectations and a recovery path. Complete the written book tasks is an overwhelming assignment. Show one activity at a time, a short estimate and Save & pause. Distinguish activity completed, self-check recorded and skill demonstrated. Native audio links need precise references and return paths. Acceptance: Day 1 requires no outside instructions, every assessed item was taught, all required lessons have four explained examples/three drills/fresh blind work, reload restores exact step/draft, disabled completion names missing tasks, and every day has substantive original content.

## Response B

Prioritize depth and reuse over extra navigation. Build an objective-to-lesson coverage map. Every book objective needs original teaching, practice and fresh transfer inside the site. Explicitly cover currently shallow quantities, informal times, irregular verbs, articles/plurals, pronouns, commands and participles. No required teaching goal should depend on opening the PDF.

Start each day with mixed retrieval from approximately 1, 3, 7 and 14 days earlier. Revisit errors with changed contexts; every core pattern should have three later retrieval opportunities. Distinguish assisted practice from independent success. Use practical missions: read opening hours, hear an appointment, write or say a response. Fade frames before the final attempt. Each chapter needs an unfamiliar scenario combining two skills; final-week missions combine three or four. Teach listening as gist, details, transcript reveal, replay and imitation. Practise spelling, number discrimination, stress and difficult sound contrasts. Label synthetic speech. Make confidence evidence visible: next action, stopping point, saved work, completed versus recalled later versus self-checked speech/writing. Reload should restore position and drafts; compare earlier and later attempts without unsupported automated grading.

## Response C

Audit coverage before expanding the calendar. Build a matrix of grammar, functions, vocabulary, pronunciation and strategies to original teaching, practice, transfer and scheduled day. Each row needs concrete IDs; PDF work is supplementary.

Build one complete chapter, then the remaining eleven. Use stable objective/lesson/exercise/day IDs with content revisions. Each lesson needs two passes with two examples, three drills, theory, reading, writing, listening, speaking and fresh blind work. Use 24 instruction, four review and seven application/repair/mock days with explicit 180-minute budgets. Migrate evidence first: current core.clean drops unknown IDs and sprint.clean uses day numbers. Preserve the 46 old lessons, drafts, vocabulary reviews, settings and timer. Archive the 21-day schedule under a plan ID; only transfer unchanged activity evidence. Back up storage before idempotent migration. Strengthen gates: core.finish checks drills and blindCorrect but not teaching passes or skill evidence. Store activity IDs/attempt metadata and use fresh variants. Distinguish self-assessed speech/writing and synthetic/native audio. Extend tests for coverage, prerequisites, negative gates, unseen-task separation, realistic migration, drafts, import/export and corrupt-state recovery; browser-check keyboard/mobile/resume/Start/black UI/credits. Reject calendar-only expansion and proficiency inferred from completion.

## Response D

Full A1 means completing target everyday interactions with unfamiliar details, understanding short accessible messages and producing useful speech/writing. Lesson completion is participation, not proof of ability.

Build an outcome ledger. Each book communicative goal, grammar feature, vocabulary set, pronunciation target and strategy needs an outcome, prerequisites, teaching, practice, assessment and later retrieval. A focus field or PDF assignment does not count. Each manageable outcome receives two teaching passes with two examples each, three drills and fresh blind transfer. The second pass should re-explain the same idea differently. Vocabulary needs a finite inventory with article/plural, useful verb forms and phrases in context, assessed through comprehension and production. Immediate success must transfer to changed details; revisit after roughly 1, 3 and 7 days, with failure-specific repair. Reading/listening checks need unseen material; keep listening transcripts closed initially and clearly label synthetic/native audio. Save writing/speaking evidence against task-specific rubrics, not only word counts and checkboxes. Distinguish self-assessment from external assessment. Current daily retrieval recycles lesson questions. Sequence prerequisites then practical interactions. Treat 35 days as a planning envelope; Start should open the next useful task, including repair. Preserve progress/drafts but distinguish previously completed lessons from newly demonstrated outcomes.

## Response E

Renaming the schedule is not expansion. Textbook.js delegates the rest of the chapter to book assignments; each capsule adds four glossary items and one narrow blind sentence. Require every book grammar point, function, vocabulary set, pronunciation target and strategy to map to actual explanation, practice and assessment. Chapter titles/page links do not count.

Current completion manufactures confidence: exact-model writing rejects valid alternatives and accepts copying. Immediate sentence substitution does not establish transfer. Require fresh item banks, delayed recall, varied contexts and error-specific repair. Keep completed lessons, unassisted retrieval and independently assessed speech/writing separate. Review has a sampling defect: flattening paired chapter questions and taking three omits part of the second chapter. Explicitly assess all reviewed chapters; timed mocks need unseen tasks. Listening/speaking need original dialogues, comprehension, delayed transcripts, sound contrasts and concrete rubrics; browser speech cannot certify pronunciation. Numeric day migration can silently reinterpret old work. Preserve old records/drafts under a stable plan ID. Three hours must be resumable small blocks with one next action and no backlog pile-up. Accept only when every day has concrete content, realistic timing and a recoverable stopping point.
