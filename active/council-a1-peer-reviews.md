# Peer reviews

## Reviewer 1
Strongest D: separates participation from demonstrated ability, changed contexts, delayed retrieval, failure-specific repair and task evidence. Biggest blind spot A: completion counts can coexist with weak retention. All missed assessment validation: documented scoring, accepted valid alternatives, calibrated human review of sample speech/writing, and unseen tasks beyond familiar-template substitutions. Baseline and comparable delayed assessments help distinguish prior knowledge and retained learning; report narrow observations until validated.

## Reviewer 2
Strongest D: usable outcomes, prerequisites, finite vocabulary, meaningful second teaching pass. Biggest blind spot A: usability cannot establish finite comprehensive scope. All missed actual book-grounded inventory and dependency sequence; controlled-language policy should prevent hidden vocabulary or grammar prerequisites in blind work. Validate workload with real beginner attempts, including retrieval/repair/breaks. Planned scope completion differs from retained A1 ability.

## Reviewer 3
Strongest A: concrete one-activity UX, exact resume, expectations, recovery and Day 1 no-external-instructions test. Biggest blind spot C: every micro-lesson containing every skill risks an exhausting checklist. All missed sustainability of 180 focused minutes for an ADHD beginner. Bound the essential day, make extensions optional, add breaks/buffer and welcoming restart. Validate timing against real beginner use; estimates are not guarantees.

## Reviewer 4
Strongest C: stable IDs, migration, evidence preservation, gates and tests. Biggest blind spot C: requiring everything in every lesson can be unbuildable and exhausting. All missed specific browser/migration cases: realistic legacy fixtures migrate twice identically without day reinterpretation; each missing requirement blocks completion; blind differs from teaching/drills; narrow mobile, keyboard obstruction, explicit audio start, no speech service, denied microphone; interrupted saves/corrupt imports/storage failure; actual timed beginner Day 1 trial.

## Reviewer 5
Strongest D: practical communication, outcome separation, prerequisites, unseen comprehension, delayed recall and rubrics. Biggest blind spot A: links/drafts/gates do not establish productive competence. All missed the feedback cycle attempt → diagnosis → short explanation → revision → fresh reattempt. Define valid alternative answers and uncertainty behavior. Speaking needs contingent clarification/repair, not just monologues. Define listening conditions, skill scoring anchors, independent item pools; pilot workload/audio/feedback with real beginners.
